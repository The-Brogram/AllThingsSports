Building a parlay

  • Two or three legs. Each extra leg multiplies the risk: three 75% legs land together only about 42% of the time if independent.
  • Each leg at 70% or better from the Most likely tier, and no questionable players or QB-change teams.
  • Use the joint estimate, not the product. Same-game legs move together. A QB yardage over and his WR1 over are positively correlated; two receivers on one team partly compete for the same targets.
  • Price check: a parlay is worth taking only if its payout beats the fair odds of the joint estimate.
